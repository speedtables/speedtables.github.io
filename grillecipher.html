Turning Grille Cipher

Build a grille, then encrypt or decrypt by rotating it four times.

Choose one open square from each number group.

White squares are open holes. Shaded squares are closed. For odd sizes, the centre square is not used.

Type a message, choose encrypt or decrypt, and use the grille on the left. The result is read row by row, skipping the centre square on odd grids.

Action

Letters are used. Spaces and punctuation are ignored for the grille.

Valid Grille Helper

Squares with the same number are the same place after rotation. Click squares here to open or close them. A valid grille has exactly one open square from each number group.
